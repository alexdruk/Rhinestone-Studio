# Rhinestone Studio 2.1.0

Image → Strass now has the improvements PatternNifty made to it, a Black & white look and a tinted
view, and the layout service is easier to start. Two new export formats: EPS and AI.

## New

- **Black & white.** An Image → Strass layer can be Colour (the default, and every older layer) or
  Black & white, in two styles: Bold (solid areas) and Shaded (Jet and Crystal mixed). Choose the
  look in the lightbox above the file button. Black & white uses only Jet and Crystal.
- **Tinted view.** "Background: Tinted" on the 2D canvas shows image layers as the finished piece:
  gaps in a darker shade of the stones around them. It is a view only. Nothing is saved, and
  exports are unchanged.
- **Tinted result in the lightbox.** A redraw now ends on the Tinted view. Template (the plain
  stones) and Source (the photo) are a click away.
- **EPS and AI exports.** In the Export dialog under DXF, for every product. Both are made from
  the final stone layout.
- **`npm run studio`.** Starts the layout service and the app together, and stops both on Ctrl+C.
  It reuses the layout service if it already runs, and refuses to start next to another service
  on the same port.
- **Layout service status in the lightbox.** The lightbox shows whether the layout service is
  Ready, Busy, Starting, Not running or the wrong service, with a Check button. With `npm run dev`
  it starts a service that is not running when the lightbox opens, and offers "Start layout
  service" if that fails.
- **Overlap cleanup and hole fill.** After the layout, the service removes stones that sit closer
  than 0.1 mm and fills holes inside the design with SS6 and SS4 stones, coloured from their
  neighbours.
- **Pale skin.** Small groups of white or gold stones on lit skin take the nearest skin colour,
  when most of their neighbours are light skin stones.

## Changed

- **Faces are found with MediaPipe** (Apache 2.0) instead of dlib, whose face model may not be
  used commercially. Pupils are the same on the test images.
- **Face rules from PatternNifty.** People's eyes get no iris ring, skin shadows stay warm, and
  single white stones on skin take a skin colour. Some stones near people's pupils move.
- **Prompt version 7.** The AI keeps each person's skin tone and lightness.
- **Flat Sheet.** The AI image takes the shape of the uploaded picture instead of a square, and
  the AI sees only the cut-out. Vessels and the plate are unchanged.
- **Stone size cap.** No stone is larger than SS16, except a pair of pupils (SS20).
- **The layout service runs on port 8001** (8000 before). Set `LAYOUT_PORT` to change it.
- **Redraw button.** While the layout service is starting, the redraw button waits. While it is
  not running, the button says "(old method)".
- The "layout service unavailable" messages say how to fix it.

## Fixed

- Inch production sheets printed "Gap: 0 in" for a 0.1 mm gap. They now print
  "Gap: 0.1 mm (0.004 in)".
- The layout service crashed when a dark round area touched the edge of the image.
- A face was found in a lake landscape, so 20 Jet stones turned Smoked Topaz. They now stay Jet.

## Known issues

- **No design fits the mug.** AI designs start at about 130 mm and the mug's body is 95 mm. The
  bottle takes only the smaller designs.
- **Remove the sample text before a redraw.** On a new project, stones of the design that overlap the sample text are removed. The design can also be larger than the 150 × 150 mm sheet; the canvas grows to fit it.
- **No warm reddish mid-brown stone.** The lit areas of deep skin go to Light Peach and look too
  light.
- **Thin eye and lip lines are lost.** The eyes become single dots. It shows most in Black & white
  Bold.
- **White patches on light skin.** Groups of Crystal and Silver stones on skin highlights read as
  white patches, most in the Tinted view.
- **Grey rings on Jet in the Tinted view.** Large black areas show a light-grey edge on every
  stone.
- **Pupils are capped.** A single pupil is at most SS16, a pair SS20.
- **The hole fill closes some gaps between curls** that the AI image shows white.
- **The Tinted view was not timed on Chrome 103**, only on a newer Chrome.
- **Bold and Shaded give different sizes.** For the same photo, Shaded comes out 11 to 21 % larger
  than Bold, because the AI draws more stones across in Shaded.
- **An animal can be taken for a person.** The face rules then act on its fur.
- **The full layout service test suite takes about 4.5 hours.**
